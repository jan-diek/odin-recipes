Soygurt with Berries

Description

It does not get much simpler than this. Nonetheless this is a healthy snack. Let your taste or the season decide which kinds of berries to use. They can be fresh or frozen.

Ingredients

  • 250g soy yogurt
  • 50g berries

Steps

  1. Fill the soy yogurt in a bowl and add the berries on top.
  2. If the berries are frozen, wait a few minutes before eating.
