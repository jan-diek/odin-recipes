Lasagna

Description

The lasagna is a traditional italian dish. It consists of layers of pasta plates, bechamel, and bolognese. Here we explore a vegan variant.

Ingredients

  • lasagna pasta plates
  • 2 Tbsp flour
  • 2 Tbsp margarine
  • 250ml soy milk
  • 200g vegan mince
  • 400ml canned tomatoes
  • salt, pepper, nutmeg

Steps

  1. For the bechamel, melt the margarine in a pot. Then mix in the flour. Slowly add the soy milk while stirring. Bowl for 2min. Season with salt and nutmeg.
  2. For the bolognese, stirfry the mince. Add the tomatoes and mash them. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Layer everything in a form: bolognese, pasta, bechamel, bolognese, pasta, ... Make sure to end with bechamel on top. Bake for 20min at 200°C.
